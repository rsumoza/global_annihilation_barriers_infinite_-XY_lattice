Suggested alt text:

Two conceptual panels explain the separating atlas. The left panel shows
overlapping coordinate rectangles, a shaded separating region containing
the dipolar minimum, and two exact cut lines through the saddles. A
covered coordinate face lies inside a neighbor, while retained outer
walls require energy bounds. An illustrative upper path crosses at one
saddle. The right panel shows a coordinate face and an orange allowed
low-energy fiber interval strictly inside a blue neighboring transverse
ball. The interval only represents a slice; a Hilbert-norm inequality
certifies inclusion of the complete infinite-dimensional fiber ball.
The figure contains no numerical certificate coordinates.

Mathematical caveats:

1. Only a conceptual rank-two reaction-coordinate projection is shown in
   panel (a), with all Y fibers omitted. Shapes and proportions are not
   the 109-chart data and are not energy contours.
2. Exact planes have normals r_j in ran Q, so their equation is constant
   along each Y fiber. The cap proof applies in the full exact plane;
   the short thick segments are only planar sections of those caps.
3. The orange curve is a schematic variational upper path, not a
   heteroclinic orbit or a plotted computed path.
4. The test in panel (b) covers the full permitted sublevel ball, not
   necessarily the entire chart ball of radius R_i. The coordinate
   cell and all its boundary points must also be strictly inside A_j.
5. The norm inequality, rather than its two-dimensional visualization,
   certifies the required infinite-dimensional inclusion.
