2. FULL-FIBER CAP CONTRACT IS THE RIGHT ONE

It encloses the orthogonal squared distance from the chart center to the saddle candidate using G and Ydist2, then adds the exact-root uncertainty rho and beta/mu. The strict bound below Rcap is the cap-lifting criterion. The checker does not require the entire fiber ball to fit into the local cap, which would be needlessly restrictive; strong convexity lifts the bound from its minimizer to every point of the fiber. This is exactly the analytic argument in geometry_lemmas.tex lines79–99 and cap_surgery.tex lines249–303.
The truncation d2.lo=max(0,d2.lo) at line51 is safe because the represented squared distance is nonnegative. If d2.hi<0 the interval constructor fails rather than silently accepting the cell.
