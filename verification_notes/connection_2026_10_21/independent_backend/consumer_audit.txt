Compiled-literal dependencies requiring explicit closure
-------------------------------------------------------
barrier/certify.cpp line 9 hardcodes rho=.017422L, delta=.001166548L.
barrier/certify.cpp line 55 hardcodes mustar=.05825L.
Padded comparisons to the corresponding exact decimal strings are almost,
but not literally, comparisons to these compiled binary constants. Record
raw baseline rho <= compiled rho, raw baseline delta <= compiled delta,
and raw baseline mu >= compiled mustar. Their actual large margins can be
verified exactly from the sidecars; do not infer them merely from closeness.

There is a second, different branch dependency. The barrier uses
maxWidth < 1.12L at line 24. The stage2 supervisor's much weaker check
branch_margin-PAD > 1.11999999999999 does NOT by itself imply that 1.12L
is within the baseline certified margin. For the frozen sources it closes
by exact source identity: baseline engine.cpp chooses margin=1.12L for
L=12, and barrier uses the identical 1.12L literal, same compiler and flags.
Record this identity or the equality of their compiled binary sidecars.

The saddle's own margin=.65L is exported and padded downward before the
rational consequences; there is no separate conflicting compiled threshold
needed for that particular scalar dependency.

Decimal context and exactness
-----------------------------
stage2_verify.py parses JSON numbers directly as Decimal, then performs only
addition/subtraction of PAD and comparisons. It performs no Decimal division
or products in its acceptance tests. Its original baseline numbers have 22
significant digits, barrier numbers 24; in the observed magnitude ranges
(roughly .001 to 1.12), adding/subtracting 10^-18 requires at most 25 significant
digits. Python's default 28-digit context therefore performs these particular
operations exactly. All nonlinear consequences are recomputed with Fraction
in postprocess_certificate.py and postprocess_global.py.
The 36-/45-digit Decimal conversions in those modules are display fields;
subsequent mathematical consumers use their exact rational string fields.

Files not consumed as directed bounds
------------------------------------
connections/*.nodes.jsonl and *.segments.jsonl use unpadded 24-digit numeric
printing. stage2/barrier/results/coefficients.txt and path_grid_640.txt use
unpadded 28-digit printing. There is no mathematical read of those dumps in
the inspected current supervisor chains. They must not be described as
universally outward decimal interval endpoints solely because they print
many digits. The proof uses the in-memory interval checks and the padded
summary scalars listed above. Files can remain approximate diagnostic tables
without compromising the actual checked chain.

Conclusion and limits
---------------------
No sign reversal or missing PAD was found among the current connection
postprocessors' actual consumed summary fields. The optimality overlay has
the direct unpadded threshold comparisons listed above; they must be closed
for the fixed inputs using exact sidecars. The compiled-literal comparisons
and branch-margin identity are also explicit proof obligations, not generic
consequences of invertibility or a successful printed PASS label.
This source audit does not independently certify residuals, Hessians, tails,
normalization, or the mathematical global separation argument.
