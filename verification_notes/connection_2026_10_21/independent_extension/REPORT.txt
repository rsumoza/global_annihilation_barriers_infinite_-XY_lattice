Independent common-normal check
------------------------------
check_normal_exact.py is independently coded using exact binary64 dyadics,
Python integers and the degree-four Laplacian of zero extensions. The raw
Q and q hashes and all boundary zeros are checked. With

 G=Q*Q, g=Q*q, alpha=G^{-1}g, d=g^T alpha,
 n=-g/sqrt(d), c=-alpha/sqrt(d),

the identities Gc=n and c^T Gc=1 are checked by rational numerators. It is
not inferred from overlapping normal and adjoint interval outputs. Both
normal coordinate enclosures and both adjoint coefficient enclosures are
verified for both reflections (eight inclusions). Sign-aware rational
square comparisons avoid any approximate square root. All passed, also
under Python -O. This independent result is restricted to these fixed
coordinates; it does not prove the Poisson residual or cap coercivity.
