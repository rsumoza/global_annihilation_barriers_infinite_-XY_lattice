on X modulo constants. For fixed u, DE_b(u) is a bounded linear functional.
The unique minimizer of R(v)+DE_b(u)[v] satisfies
<v,w>_X=-DE_b(u)[w], hence v=-grad_X E_b(u), with the printed minus sign.
The weak form is equivalent to D*D dotu=-D*(J sin Du), where D* is used
distributionally; it does not assert an l2 vertex Laplacian inverse.
