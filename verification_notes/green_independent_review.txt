1. Multiplier and normalization.

For forward bonds D_i theta(x)=theta(x+e_i)-theta(x), the gradient projector
on l2(Z2;R2) has multiplier m_ij=d_i conjugate(d_j)/lambda, with
d_i=exp(ik_i)-1 and lambda=sum_i|d_i|2. There is no missing factor2:
each positive bond is counted once and m_11+m_22=1 almost everywhere.
The normalization dk/(2pi)^2 is correct. Orthogonal projection gives
||P||=1 and stationarity yields P sin(Dtheta)=0 in the closed gradient
space. Thus Dtheta=P(Dtheta-sin Dtheta), without an l2 inverse for the
vertex Laplacian.

The bare Green multiplier1/lambda is not integrable at zero in2D.
The proof never integrates it over both variables alone: it first fixes
the other variable !=0 and then multiplies the partially integrated
coefficients by the edge symbols. Fubini applies directly to bounded
m_ij. The exceptional fixed-variable value zero has measure zero.

2. Independent derivation of the kernel bound.

Choose the Fourier variable corresponding to a largest component n of z,
r=|n|=||z||infty. Let t denote the other variable and
s=|sin(t/2)|, alpha=2asinh(s). Then cosh(alpha)=2-cos(t) and

  g_n(t)=(1/2pi) integral exp(iny)/lambda(t,y) dy
        =exp(-alpha|n|)/(2sinh(alpha)), t!=0.

For diagonal entries at r>=1, partial integration has magnitude
s exp(-alpha r)/sqrt(1+s2). The other diagonal entry is its negative
because the relevant Kronecker delta is zero when n!=0.

For mixed entries, the coefficients are d_other(g_{n+1}-g_n) or its
conjugate with the opposite shift. Exactly, their magnitude is at most

  s exp[-alpha(r-1/2)]/sqrt(1+s2).

This independent formula explicitly tracks the potentially adverse
one-step anchor shift, including n=+/-1. It already suffices for the
claimed constant8, using |P_ij(z)|<=1/2 for r=0,1 and direct integration
for r>=2.

The author's alternate bound is also valid and simpler for uniform r>=1:

  |g_{n+/-1}-g_n|
  <=exp(-alpha r)(exp(alpha)-1)/(2sinh alpha)
  =exp(-alpha r) exp(alpha)/(exp(alpha)+1)
  <=exp(-alpha r).

Multiplication by |d_other|=2s bounds both mixed coefficients by
2s exp(-alpha r). The equality involving the exponential quotient is
correct; there is no lost exp(alpha) at r=1.

For |t|<=pi, alpha>=sqrt2 |t|/pi and s<=|t|/2. Hence

  (1/2pi) integral s exp(-alpha r) dt <=pi/(4r2),
  |P_ij(z)|<=pi/(2r2)<2/r2<=8/(1+r)2, r>=1.

At z=0 the bounded multiplier gives |P_ij(0)|<=1, sufficient for8.
Coordinate exchange preserves the orientation indexing and covers the
case where the other component realizes ||z||infty. Therefore

  |P_ij(z)|<=8/(1+||z||infty)2

is established with no hidden endpoint translation.

3. Column tail and recurrence.

There are8r anchor vertices on shell r>=1 and2 output orientations.
For integer rho>=1:

  sum_{||z||>=rho,i}|P_ij(z)|2
  <=1024 sum_{r>=rho} r/(1+r)4
  <=1024 integral_rho^infty t^(-3)dt=512/rho2.

Thus the column-tail l2 norm is <=16sqrt2/rho, already summed over
both output orientations. Minkowski over scalar source entries requires
no additional sqrt2. Splitting q by source anchors |y|<R/2 and >=R/2,
for even R>=2R*, gives separation >=R/2 and hence

  T(R)<=32sqrt2 ||q||1/R+a T(R/2), a=delta2/6.

The coefficient a multiplies the *source* tail; applying the full l2
projection contraction before target restriction is valid. The source
core is finite, while q in l1 ensures the required sums converge. The
global Taylor inequality |t-sin t|<=|t|3/6 and l3<=l2 yield
||q||1<=||theta||X3/6. No small-angle assumption is imposed in the core.

For R_n=2^n R*, the invariant bound

  A>=R*T(R*), A>=32sqrt2 Q/(1-2a)
  implies T(R_n)<=A/R_n

by induction. For arbitrary R between dyadic radii the safe bound is
2A/R, not A/R. The stated safe rational replacements32sqrt2<46 and
sqrt227<16 are valid.

5. Corrected coupling extension.

For J_e=1+b_e, ||b||1<=beta and an exact critical theta of E_J, define

  q_b=Dtheta-(1+b)sin(Dtheta).

Then q_b is in l1 and
Q_b<=||theta||X3/6+beta, while stationarity gives Dtheta=Pq_b.
Where |Dtheta|<=eta, the exact pointwise inequality is

  |q_b,e|<= (eta2/6+||b||infty)|Dtheta_e|
           <=(eta2/6+beta)|Dtheta_e|.

Thus a_b=eta2/6+beta<1/2 suffices for the same recurrence and1/R tail.
No common support, spatial moment, or tail modulus on b is necessary
for this small multiplicative-coupling setting. Treating b as a generic
additive force would lose this key cancellation.

6. Numerical scope and recommendation.

The coefficients in the stated budgets are conservative consequences of
M=6007, not measurements of the actual q norm. They are tail-error
budgets, not newly computed minimax barriers. Replacing thousands of
radius digits by20-25 digits is a genuine proved asymptotic improvement
but does not make the full-grid minimax algorithm practical. Preserve
that distinction in abstract, theorem applications and conclusion.

The manuscript may assert the explicit projector estimate, the1/R
critical-tail theorem and the corrected small-coupling extension.
It should attribute general lattice Green/defect decay to the existing
literature rather than claim first decay theory. Its additional value
is the explicit constants and integration with the global certificate.
