PRACTICAL LOCALIZATION DISCUSSION
The established truncation estimate is C_E/R. Thus a target error epsilon
requires R on the order of C_E/epsilon. The dyadic parametrization
R=4097*2^n does not make the radius exponential in inverse accuracy.
The severe exponential growth is the cost of a complete cubical search
in the number of retained degrees of freedom (of order R^2 vertices).

Specific plausible improvements, to label as future obligations rather
than achieved results:
- Enclose the nonlinear source q=Dtheta-sin(Dtheta) directly in a finite
  certified core and estimate its exterior separately, replacing the
  crude global M^3/6 majorant by data-dependent sums.
- Use signed kernel differences and subtract verified leading moments
  before taking absolute values, with a rigorous remainder for the
  lattice Green expansion. Neutral total vortex charge alone is not a
  proof that the nonlinear source has a vanishing vector moment.
- Retain analytic exterior control and the finite reaction atlas rather
  than materialize a full state-space grid.
- Recheck any stronger moment or symmetry assumption uniformly in the
  admitted coupling class. The present ell1 family allows noncompact,
  spatially translated heterogeneities; a moment hypothesis cannot be
  silently added to that class.

No theorem in the manuscript establishes a multiphase averaging method.
Averaging phase configurations is not automatically compatible with real
lifts, charges, the endpoint set, or a fixed energy ceiling. Presenting
such averaging as a proved shortcut would overstate the current result.
