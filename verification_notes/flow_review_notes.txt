The source states the current conservation law as a sum of shunt normal,
Josephson supercurrent, and noise terms. Equation13 contains the factor
hbar/(2eR) times d(phi_i-phi_j)/dt and Ic sin(phi_i-phi_j). Its equations
14–15 add fluctuating twists; the manuscript must not claim equivalence
to that full driven system merely by citing Eq13.

Mathematical derivation (independent of the citation):
R(v)=1/2||Dv||_2^2 on X. Minimizing R(v)+DE_b(u)[v] gives the X-Riesz
gradient flow and the exact energy law. In a finite graph with grounded
boundary (or constants quotiented), KCL for homogeneous shunt conductance
gives the same stiffness operator D*D multiplying dotu. Different Josephson
couplings J_e can coexist with fixed equal shunt conductance; the theorem
does not assert any microscopic relationship between the two parameters.

Scope to emphasize:
- The metric controls a specified zero-drive deterministic relaxation law.
- The paper certifies defect annihilation, not a thermodynamic phase transition.
- Static minimax depends on energy AND admissible topology. Do not claim
  independence from arbitrary changes of infinite-dimensional path topology.
- Same path class/energy and a different mobility yield the same variational
  barrier by definition, but not automatically the same orbit or rate.
- Infinite weak formulation is the model; no finite-to-infinite circuit limit
  or bounded inverse of the l2 vertex Laplacian is used.
