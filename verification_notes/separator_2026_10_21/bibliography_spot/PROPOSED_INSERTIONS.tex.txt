For general dislocation predictors, force-balance conditions also require
verification: Braun and Hudson \cite{BraunHudson2026} clarify the vanishing
of the linearised force sum and the consequences of its failure. The
finite-energy tail argument below instead starts from an exact gradient
projection identity with an absolutely summable bond source.

% 2. Green section: insert directly after the paragraph containing
% eq:green-representation, before 'For an even integer...'.
Since $q\in\ell^1(\mathcal E)$, its vertex divergence has an absolutely
convergent sum equal to zero: the two endpoint contributions of each bond
cancel. Likewise, the compact Poisson loads used by the certificate are
exact divergences of compact bond fields. These cancellations are algebraic;
they are not inferred from decay of a general dislocation predictor
\cite{BraunHudson2026}.
